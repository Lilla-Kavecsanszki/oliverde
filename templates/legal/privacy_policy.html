We process personal data for the following purposes:

Depending on the nature of your enquiry, we process your personal data on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests: where it is necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business correspondence, operate the website securely and protect our business.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is required by applicable law.

5. How enquiries are handled

Information submitted through the contact form is stored within the website database and may also be transmitted by email to authorised Oliverde personnel so that the enquiry can be reviewed and answered.

We use MailerSend as an email-delivery service. MailerSend may process the information contained in your enquiry solely to transmit the relevant email on our behalf.

6. Service providers and recipients

Personal data may be processed by carefully selected service providers that support the operation of this website and act on our instructions.

These may include providers of:

  • email delivery;
  • media and content delivery;

The website currently uses Heroku for hosting, PostgreSQL for database storage, MailerSend for email delivery and Cloudinary for the storage and delivery of website images.

Access to personal data is limited to authorised persons and service providers who require it for the purposes described in this policy. We do not sell personal data.

Some of our technology providers may process or store information outside Italy or the European Economic Area.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, we require an appropriate legal transfer mechanism and safeguards in accordance with applicable data-protection law, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards.

8. How long we keep personal data

Enquiries and related correspondence are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, manage any resulting business relationship, protect our legitimate interests and comply with legal obligations.

Enquiries that do not result in a contractual relationship will ordinarily be deleted or anonymised within 24 months of the last meaningful communication, unless a longer period is required for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in the contact form.
