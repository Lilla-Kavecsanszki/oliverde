Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

1. About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the Oliverde website uses cookies and similar browser technologies.

The website does not currently use advertising cookies, profiling cookies or analytics cookies.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device when you visit it. They can support essential functions such as security, session management and remembering technical preferences.

3. Cookies used by this website

Oliverde uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for the security and operation of the website.

Cookie Provider Purpose Typical duration
csrftoken Oliverde Helps protect website forms against cross-site request forgery and other unauthorised submissions. Up to one year
sessionid Oliverde Maintains a secure session where session functionality is required, including restricted administrative areas. Up to two weeks

A particular cookie will only be placed where the corresponding website function requires it.

4. Consent

Strictly necessary cookies do not require prior consent because the website cannot provide the relevant secure functionality without them.

For this reason, Oliverde does not currently display a cookie-consent banner.

5. Analytics and advertising

The website does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, behavioural profiling or cross-site tracking technologies.

Should non-essential cookies or tracking technologies be introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and an appropriate consent mechanism will be implemented before those technologies are used.

6. Third-party website services

Images and other technical resources may be delivered through service providers used to operate the website. These services are not used by Oliverde for advertising or behavioural profiling.

The site's fonts are hosted locally and are not downloaded from Google Fonts when you visit the website.

7. Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through their privacy settings.

Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent forms, sessions or security features from functioning correctly.
