How it works

From first conversation to complete peace of mind

  1. 01

    Tell us about your home

    A first conversation about the property, how you use it and what matters most to you, whether you are in residence for months or only a few days a year.

  2. 02

    We visit and plan

    We visit the property, review the house, gardens, pool and local arrangements, and propose a care plan built around the way you live.

  3. 03

    Your team is put in place

    We coordinate trusted local specialists and contractors, with Oliverde as your single point of contact for everything.

  4. 04

    Ongoing care, clear updates

    Regular inspections and photographic updates keep you informed, and the house is prepared before every arrival so you can simply return and enjoy it.
