Your dashboard

Everything about your home, in one place

Every owner has a private online dashboard, shared through Google Drive. It gathers the whole story of your property, so you can look in from anywhere, at any time, without having to ask.

Your property at a glance

An overview of the house, its systems and appliances, with simple notes on how everything works, and emergency contacts and trusted local specialists close to hand.

Regular checks, recorded

Indoor and outdoor checklists covering the house, grounds, utilities and security, with periodic readings and a record of any issue noticed.

Servicing and upkeep

A service history for heating, cooling, appliances and other equipment, so you know what was done, and when it is next due.

Clear costs

Running costs, a log of the work carried out, and a costed record of projects, so you can see where every euro has gone.

Taxes and deadlines

Annual property taxes (IMU and TARI) and their due dates are tracked for you, so nothing arrives as a surprise.

Photos, quotes and invoices

An organised archive, filed by date, of photographs, videos, quotes and invoices. If you let your home, a bookings tracker can sit alongside it.
